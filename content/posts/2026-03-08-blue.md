As all the things turn blue
let me stay awhile with you
through the hush of falling light
where the day dissolves from view

hold the air and hold it near
like a song half lost, half true
when the world forgets its name
I remember it through you

if night should reach too soon
if the stars are pale and few
let my wandering heart be still
as all the things turn blue
